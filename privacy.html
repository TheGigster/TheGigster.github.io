Privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Gig is a personal assistant used only by its developer, Cam, with his own Google accounts. This policy explains what Gig does with Google user data.

Data Gig accesses

  • Gmail (read-only access and permission to create drafts): message metadata, subjects, short previews and, when Cam asks, the text of an email.
  • Google Calendar (events): event titles, times, locations and attendees, plus creating an event after Cam confirms it.

How it is used

  • To answer Cam's questions, summarise and sort his email, flag what needs his attention, prepare drafts for him to review, and remind him about upcoming events.
  • Gig never sends email, never deletes email or events, and never shares or sells Google user data.
  • Gig does not use Google user data for advertising, and does not use it to train general-purpose AI models.

Where it is stored

  • Sign-in tokens and short summaries are stored only on Cam's own computers, in files that only his account can read. Full email bodies are not stored.
  • To understand a request, the relevant text may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models. Much of the sorting runs on a local model on Cam's own computer, so that text never leaves it.

Limited Use

Gig's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing Gig's files from the computer it runs on deletes everything it stored.
