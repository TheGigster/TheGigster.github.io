Gig

A private personal assistant for one person.

Gig is a personal assistant that Cam built for his own use. It runs on his own computers and helps him keep up with his work and personal email, calendar and messages.

What it does with Google

  • Gmail: reads and searches Cam's email so it can summarise it and tell him what needs his attention, and creates drafts for him to review. It never sends email by itself and never deletes anything.
  • Google Calendar: reads Cam's events, and adds an event only after he says yes to that exact event.

Who can use it

Gig is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up, and it only ever connects to Cam's own Google accounts.
